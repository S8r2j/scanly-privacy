Privacy Policy for Scanly

Last updated: May 03, 2026

1. Introduction

Scanly ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Scanly mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information:

a) Camera Access

  • We access your device camera ONLY for QR code scanning
  • Camera images are processed locally and NOT stored or transmitted
  • We do not record, save, or upload any photos or videos

b) Scan History

  • QR codes you scan and generate are stored LOCALLY on your device
  • We do NOT upload or transmit your scan history to any servers
  • You can delete your history at any time within the app

c) Advertising Data (via Google AdMob)

  • Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data for ad personalization
  • See Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • We do not control or access this data

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Camera: For QR code scanning only
  • Scan History: Stored locally for your convenience
  • Ads: To provide free app access with ad-supported revenue

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal information.

Third-party services we use:

  • Google AdMob (for advertising)

5. Data Security

  • All scan history is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage
  • Camera access is only active during scanning
  • No data is transmitted to external servers

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Clear your scan history anytime (in-app)
  • Disable camera access (device settings → Apps → Scanly → Permissions)
  • Opt-out of personalized ads (device settings → Google → Ads)

7. Children's Privacy

Scanly is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of Scanly constitutes acceptance of changes.
