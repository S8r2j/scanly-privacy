This Privacy Policy describes how Veyonlab ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Scanly mobile application ("App") available on the Google Play Store. By using Scanly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary: Scanly does not create accounts, does not upload your scan data to any server, and does not sell your personal information to anyone. All scan history is stored only on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Scanly is designed with privacy in mind. We collect the minimum information necessary to provide the app's features.

a) Information stored locally on your device:

  • Scan history: QR codes and barcodes you scan are saved locally on your device using the app's internal storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it manually.
  • Generated QR codes: Any QR codes you create within the app are stored locally on your device only.
  • App preferences: Settings and preferences you configure are stored locally.

b) Information collected automatically:

  • Advertising data: Scanly uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve relevant ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Crash reports: If the app crashes, anonymous diagnostic data may be collected by the device's operating system or Google Play Services to help us improve stability.

c) Information we do NOT collect:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information
  • We do not upload your scan history or QR codes to any server
  • We do not track your location beyond what is required for WiFi functionality
  • We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties

2. Permissions We Request

Scanly requests the following device permissions and uses them solely for the stated purposes:

  • Camera: Required to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera feed is processed entirely on-device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Internet: Required to load advertisements via Google AdMob and to open scanned URLs in a browser.
  • Access Network State: Used to check network connectivity status.
  • Access WiFi State: Used to read the current WiFi connection status when connecting to a scanned WiFi network.
  • Change WiFi State: Used to assist in connecting to a WiFi network scanned from a QR code.
  • Access Fine Location: Required by Android OS to access WiFi network information. We do not track or store your physical location.
  • Change Network State: Used to manage network connections when connecting to a scanned WiFi network.
  • Post Notifications: Used to display a notification showing WiFi credentials when you choose to connect to a scanned WiFi network. Notifications are only shown when you initiate a WiFi connection.

3. How We Use Information

The limited information we access is used exclusively to:

  • Provide core app functionality — scanning, generating, and saving QR codes
  • Display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob to keep the app free
  • Improve app performance and fix technical issues
  • Display WiFi credentials in a notification to assist you in connecting to a network

We do not use your information for profiling, marketing, or any purpose beyond operating the App.

4. Third-Party Services

Scanly integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Used to serve in-app advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised ads through your device's ad settings.
  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution and crash reporting. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

All data generated by your use of Scanly (scan history, generated QR codes, preferences) is stored exclusively on your device using Android's secure local storage. We do not operate servers that store your personal data.

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your information within the App. However, no method of storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device.

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by clearing the app's data through your device's Settings → Apps → Scanly → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the App.

6. Children's Privacy

Scanly is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us immediately at the email below and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to access: Since all data is stored on your device, you can view it directly within the App at any time.
  • Right to deletion: You can delete your scan history and generated QR codes within the App, or clear all data through device settings.
  • Right to opt out of personalised ads: You can opt out through your device's Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.
  • GDPR (EU users): If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have rights under GDPR including access, rectification, and erasure of personal data.
  • CCPA (California users): California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request deletion. We do not sell personal information.

8. Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device, retention is entirely in your control. We do not retain any of your data on external servers. Uninstalling the App will remove all locally stored data.
